Who We Are

Precision consulting for teams building the next breakthrough

BiotecMaven is a strategic biologics consultancy founded by Dr. Vibha Chauhan, a seasoned biologics scientist with more than 20 years of experience in oncology, antibody engineering, and protein design.

I help biotech startups, pharma teams, and investors make better scientific decisions — supporting therapeutic programs from early discovery through preclinical development. Increasingly, I extend that biology-first expertise with computational biology and AI, applied where they genuinely add insight rather than as a separate practice.

Deep domain expertise across antibody engineering, protein design, cell line strategy, and scientific due diligence.
Better decisions, earlier — helping teams make confident scientific and strategic decisions earlier in development.
