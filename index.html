When Experimental Work Is Needed

Strategy that can move into execution.

BiotecMaven can coordinate work through qualified external laboratory partners rather than asking clients to manage multiple vendors themselves.

I remain involved in the scientific strategy: defining the question, designing the work, working with the external partner, reviewing results, troubleshooting where needed, and determining what should happen next.

External capabilities are selected for each project. Specialized expertise outside my own scope is brought in when appropriate.
